\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we introduced VANISH, an open protocol and container architecture providing cryptographically verifiable, time-bounded lifecycle enforcement for digital files. By coupling canonical CBOR metadata, Ed25519 digital signatures, and optional AES-256-GCM envelope encryption with native operating system storage monitors, VANISH bridges the longstanding gap between ephemeral messaging silos and general-purpose filesystem storage. Our prototype implementation demonstrates negligible container overhead, sub-millisecond verification latency, and robust defense against time-manipulation attacks. Future research will explore integration with trusted platform modules (TPM 2.0) and formally verified kernel drivers across consumer operating systems.
